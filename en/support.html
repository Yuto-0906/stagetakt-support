StageTakt guide

StageTakt plays backing tracks and a click for live shows from an iPhone or iPad. This page covers getting started and the questions people ask most.

Your first song

  1. In Songs, create a song and import audio from Files. M4A, AAC, FLAC, WAV and MP3 work, up to 16 files per song.
  2. Pick how the click is made: generated by the app, a click-only file, or two mixes with and without click.
  3. Enter the tempo and meter. If you don't know the BPM, use Tap tempo along with the song.
  4. Zoom into the waveform and line the click up with the first note. Choose the count-in bars and voice here too.
  5. On the Live screen, tap Prepare, then Play.

Connecting outputs

To use separate outputs you need a multi-output audio interface that works with iPhone or iPad. Some interfaces are not recognized without enough USB power.

By default, OUT 1–2 carry the backing for the PA and OUT 3–4 carry the band's mix, which adds the click and count to the backing.

Each track can be sent to any OUT at its own level. The Outputs tab also has a master level for each OUT.

Sound assigned to an OUT that isn't connected stays silent. It is never moved to a different OUT.

Before the show, check at low volume that each OUT number matches the jack you expect.

During the show

Live lock is on from the start. Touching the waveform during a song does nothing, and Pause, Stop, Next song and MUTE need a long press. Turn on Lock faders too in Settings to freeze the levels as well.

Bluetooth page-turner pedals and keyboards work: Space, ←, ↑ or Page Up to play and pause, and →, ↓ or Page Down for the next song.

If a cable comes loose or a call comes in, every track stops. Once you reconnect, the Live screen shows Prepare again from 0:00 at the point where it stopped. Tap it, then Play.

Markers let you start rehearsal from a set point in a song. Setlists hold the running order, whether to stop or continue after each song, the gap in seconds and the total running time.

Free version and Pro

The free version lets you create 3 songs and 1 setlist. Click, count-in, multiple outputs and Live lock are all included.

StageTakt Pro is a one-time purchase, with the price shown on the purchase screen in your currency. It removes the song and setlist limits and adds iCloud sync. There are no subscriptions or ads.

Buy once with an Apple Account and use Pro on both iPhone and iPad. On a new device, tap Restore Purchases in Settings. The Apple Account used for purchases can be different from the one signed in to iCloud.

If Pro ends, for example after a refund, your songs and setlists stay and still play and edit. You go back to the free limits for new items, and iCloud sync stops.

iCloud sync (Pro)

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on your iPhone and iPad, then turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings on both.

Songs, setlists and imported audio are synced. Output routing and master levels are kept separately on each device.

Nothing syncs while a song plays, while waiting after a song ends, or while an editor is open. Turn on Pause sync during the show to hold syncing until you turn it off.

Once the audio has arrived, songs prepare and play in airplane mode. A sync error never deletes data on the device.

Troubleshooting

No sound? Check the interface's power, any "OUT not connected" notice on the Live screen, and the track's MUTE and SOLO.

The count voice is recorded audio built into the app. For numbers above 20, or if you choose a device voice, the iPhone or iPad's own speech voice is used.

When you email us, include the app version, your device and iOS version, the audio interface, and what you did before the problem. Please don't send audio files or passwords.
