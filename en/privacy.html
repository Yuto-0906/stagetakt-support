Privacy Policy

This policy describes StageTakt for iPhone and iPad and this support site.

Audio and library on your device

Imported audio, song and setlist settings are stored in the app’s local storage. PCM, waveform, click and spoken-count caches are generated on the device. We do not receive your audio or library.

The app uses Apple’s on-device speech synthesis for English count-in. A voice may need to be installed or downloaded through the OS before offline use. StageTakt does not send your audio to a speech service.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable sync, song/setlist data and original audio are stored in your Apple Account’s private CloudKit database and transferred to your other devices. Apple operates iCloud under its own terms and privacy policy.

StageTakt does not operate a separate library server or provide access to your private library to the developer. Turning sync off retains both local and iCloud data.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. StageTakt uses StoreKit on the device to verify and restore the Pro purchase. We do not receive your payment-card details and do not operate a purchase-validation server.

Purchase status is not included in the library or its iCloud sync. Purchases can be restored with the same App Store Apple Account.

Advertising, analytics and permissions

StageTakt has no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK or cross-app tracking. It does not request microphone, contacts or location access.

The language preference is stored in the app’s settings. File metadata is used to verify imports, cache audio and maintain sync integrity.

Support email

When you choose to email support, the email address and the information you send are used to answer and investigate your request. Nothing is sent automatically.

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and related maintenance. You may request deletion by contacting the address below.

Retention and deletion

Deleting a song or setlist removes it from the active library and syncs that deletion. Original audio, backups and older sync revisions can remain for recovery; deleting a song is not a complete erasure of every stored copy.

To remove all local app data, delete the app rather than offloading it. To remove iCloud data, first turn sync off on every device, then use Apple’s available iCloud storage-management controls for the app. Separately created exports and device backups must be managed separately.

This website and changes

This static support site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics, advertising or external font scripts. GitHub may process access logs under its own privacy policy.

This policy will be updated when the app’s data handling changes. Contact the StageTakt developer at the address below with privacy questions.
