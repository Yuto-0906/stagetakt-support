Privacy Policy

What StageTakt and this site store, and where.

What we don't collect

StageTakt has no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no cross-app tracking. It doesn't ask for the microphone, contacts or location. The developer receives no personal information from the app.

What stays on your device

Imported audio and your song and setlist settings are stored inside the app. Playback data, waveforms, the click and the count are generated on the device. None of this is sent to the developer.

The count voice is recorded audio built into the app. For numbers above 20, or if you choose a device voice, Apple's on-device speech is used. Your audio is never sent to an outside speech service.

Settings such as language, appearance, Live lock and OUT master levels are also stored in the app on that device.

iCloud sync (Pro, only if you turn it on)

With sync on, your song and setlist settings and audio are stored in your Apple Account's private CloudKit database and delivered to your other devices. iCloud is an Apple service, covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

The developer cannot see what is in that database, and there is no separate server holding your library. Turning sync off leaves the data on the device and in iCloud as it is.

Purchases

Apple handles payment, so card details never reach the developer. The app checks the Pro purchase on the device with StoreKit. There is no separate purchase server.

Purchase information is not included in your library or in synced data.

Support email

If you email us, we use your address and message to reply and to look into the problem. We keep them as long as needed for that, and delete them if you ask. The app never sends email or logs on its own.

Feedback form (Google Forms)

The feedback form runs on Google Forms. Responses are stored in the developer's Google account and used to improve the app and to reply. Google's Privacy Policy also applies.

The form doesn't ask for your name or a sign-in. We receive an email address only if you write one to get a reply. To have a response deleted, contact us below.

Deleting data

Deleting a song or setlist removes it from your synced devices too. Audio and sync history may remain for a while so that a deletion can be undone.

To remove all app data, choose Delete App rather than Offload App. To remove data from iCloud, turn sync off on every device, then delete it from iCloud storage management in the iPhone or iPad Settings app. Exported WAV files and device backups need to be deleted separately.

About this site

This site is a set of static pages on GitHub Pages, with no analytics, ad scripts or external fonts. GitHub handles access logs under its own Privacy Statement.

If anything here changes, this page and its date will be updated.
